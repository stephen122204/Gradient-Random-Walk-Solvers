# Historical two-step heat design

`DESIGN_LOCKED_AT.txt` and `run.log` are byte-for-byte copies of the original
local study records, added here during the 2026-09-08 reproducibility pass.
The paths inside the lock refer to the original `output/heat_two_step/` folder.
The hashes match the committed copies in `pinned_ensembles/heat_two_step/`.

The lock records a local time and hashes for predictions and the validation
specification. The run log records the production progress. These are retained
records of the original work, not an external preregistration or a new timestamp
generated by the reproduction commands. A later rerun cannot establish the
chronology of an earlier experiment.

`python reproduce.py verify-two-step --pinned-only` checks both recorded hashes
and recomputes the prediction and count rule. The full verification reruns the
original seed lists in `output/heat_two_step_reproduction/` and compares every
archived numerical field. The historical validation file stores ensemble
summaries only. Fresh validation runs additionally retain per-realization
squared errors and check their mean against the reported RMS error.
